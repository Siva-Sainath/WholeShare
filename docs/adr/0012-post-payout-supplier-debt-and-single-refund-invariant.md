# 0012: Post-Payout Supplier Debt Recovery and Single-Refund Invariant

When a customer dispute is resolved in the buyer's favor after supplier payout settlement, PayPal debits the platform's balance because completed PayPal Payouts cannot be automatically reversed via API. We record the disbursed amount as an uncollected liability in `SupplierRepaymentDebt` under the external Wholesale Supplier Agreement rather than attempting unsafe automatic clawbacks or blocking legitimate buyer protection disputes. To protect both platform and supplier reserves, code strictly enforces the Single-Refund Invariant: an order can be refunded through at most one pathway (either in-app escrow refund or PayPal dispute refund), permanently blocking duplicate debits against the same capture.
